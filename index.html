Ask it to do things

Press Ctrl+K in the app (Cmd+K on a Mac) and describe what you want. The model chooses from FlashForge's own functions, runs them in order and tells you what changed. Some examples:

Make 15 cards on the causes of World War IWrites the cards into a new deck.
Make my Spanish deck harderRewrites that deck's cards with tougher prompts.
Start reviewing whatever is dueOpens a study session with today's due cards.

Instructions you save in the same box, such as "keep it short" or "I'm a beginner", are added to every AI request the app makes.

With an AI 'improve wording' pass.

Runs in your browser

Scheduling, study sessions, statistics, charts, import/export. None of this needs a network connection or a model.

Uses a language model for

JSON-mode card generation from your notes using spaced-repetition best practices. 'Why is this the answer?' tutoring grounded in the deck's original notes. On-demand mnemonic generation for stubborn cards.

Notes

Cost
Free. Each account has a daily allowance of AI requests.
Your data
What you make stays in this browser on this device. Your account is only used for signing in and for the built-in AI. You can export a backup or clear everything from Settings.
AI access
Signed-in users get the built-in AI with nothing to set up. You can also use your own Groq or OpenRouter key under Settings.
